Previously Resolved Questions

For reference, these questions have been answered in the email record:

Is the upper house ("Dwelling") connected to the system?

No. Confirmed by applicant (Apr 23, Jun 23, Jun 25, 2026), by the original site plan (provided by Carrick Palmer, Jun 24), and agreed upon by Jamie Malcolm and Carrick Palmer (Jun 25). The dwelling was never connected per the original permit. Decision: proceed without the house; add via permit modification later if needed.

What structures are currently connected?

Post Office + 2-bedroom apartment only. Specified on the revised application (Apr 6, 2026) per Jamie Malcolm's instructions (Dec 16, 2025).

What is the apartment?

The 2-bedroom apartment is part of the Post Office building. It is shown on the original 1998 site plan as "2 Bedroom Apartment and Post Office" in the same structure. There is no separate apartment building.

Is there a building behind the Post Office?

Per the 1998 site plan, there is a barn behind/below the Post Office building, near the discharge outlet. The site plan is available in the documents section below.

System capacity?

500 GPD. Corrected from initial 250 GPD submission on Dec 8, 2025.
